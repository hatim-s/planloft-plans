How to read the results

Costs cover attributed Workflow Agent authoring LLM calls, including build retries. Planning, app generation, separate agent builds, and infrastructure are outside this cost measure. Prompt-to-deploy time includes planning and connection waits.

The original prompts match. Models, cache state, connection bindings, approved workflow scope, and schedule authority differ. The observed results cannot isolate the effect of sandboxing. The repeat case was excluded from legacy summaries.

Before another comparison, verify the requested connection and schema, carry schedule permissions into the plan, and repair the terminal receipt writer. Freeze the inputs and score the same business assertions on both approaches.
