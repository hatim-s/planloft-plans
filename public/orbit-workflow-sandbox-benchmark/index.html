How to read the numbers

The sandbox totals include the 10 replays. The 10 historical runs are listed separately. Scope comes from approved assignments or linked records at readback. Linked records may include undeployed stubs. Deployment confirms a workflow was published. Business checks are separate. The historical Support run includes duplicate escalation and summarization workflow entries.
