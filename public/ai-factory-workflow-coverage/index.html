Features, high-risk refactors, and rewrites

  1. Agree on the design, compatibility checks, and rollback conditions before starting implementation.
  2. Split the work into reviewable PRs. Codex or Claude can author them. Factory reviews each exact head independently and runs focused and integration checks in disposable QA.
  3. Compare red CI against the pinned base. Rerun transient failures within a fixed budget and invalidate old evidence when the head changes.
  4. Keep product decisions, privileged access, protected merges, and production sign-off with named owners. Factory prepares the evidence for those decisions.
