Cost and token signals

Large generations

55% of output

45 of 623 LLM calls each wrote more than 5,000 tokens. Together they produced 428,908 of the 774,936 output tokens.

Calls with no cached input

$4.82 of $9.97

These calls made up 48% of combined spend. The share ranged from 34% in SharePoint to 53% in Snowflake.

Snowflake had the longest planning phase, the longest app build, and the largest output. Workflow work set the completion time in the other three runs.

App build times stayed close, from 8m 40s to 10m 5s. The two lower-cost runs should be read with their outcomes in mind: file upload was partially failed, while SharePoint succeeded.
