How to read the results

Completed means a saved and deployed workflow passed its final mocked tests. Built means a populated definition was saved; it does not certify correct behavior. Failed tests and unverifiable tests remain separate outcomes. Vendor-risk testing returned RUN_NOT_FOUND, so its result is unverified.

Structure built; data failed means a nonblank graph was saved, but required inputs, code, conditions, or mappings remained incomplete. It labels the stage reached and does not certify topology or behavioral correctness. Structure failed means only the default nodes remained.

Measurement and comparison limits

Active time sums top-level prompt-turn trace durations. Gaps between turns, including time waiting for user clarification, are excluded. Nested spans are not added again. Costs use the platform session total and were checked against the sum of per-turn trace costs. These are active authoring-session totals, including planning, building, repairs, attempted tests, and deployment; they are not isolated Builder-agent metrics.
