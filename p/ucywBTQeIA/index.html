Findings

1. 113s of untraced time on the critical path

Refresh inventory data LLM spans stop at +918s, but 8 tool calls continue to +1054s. That leaves 113.2s of untraced gaps after the last recorded LLM call, on the critical path. The trace cannot split that between real model time and stalls, so the cause is not measurable from the trace alone.

2. Output-bound calls dominate model time

7 LLM calls write 5k+ output tokens, totalling 78,672 tokens over 519s at about 142 tok/s. 106s of that sits on the critical path. A further 3 calls each took over 15s for under 300 output tokens, adding 31s to the critical path. Savings depend on the fix.

3. Half the spend is uncached

15 uncached calls cost $0.94 of the $1.88 turn, a 50% share. This is the cheapest of the three findings to act on, since it needs no change to what the agent does.

Caveats

  • The 113s untraced stretch cannot be attributed to model time versus a stall from the trace alone.

What to do next

  1. Instrument the tool-to-tool gaps in Refresh inventory data so the 113s can be split into model time and stall. Without this, any fix is a guess.
  2. Cut the 5k+ output token calls. 78,672 tokens over 519s at 142 tok/s is the largest single block of critical path time that we can see.
  3. Fix prompt caching on the 15 uncached calls, worth $0.94.
